locutius

A local meeting memory.

locutius is a program that runs on your own computer. It turns your meeting recordings into transcripts that know who spoke, notes, action lists per person, and people pages, and lets you ask questions about all of it. Everything it keeps is stored in a folder on your computer, called the workspace.

Google Calendar

If you connect Google Calendar, locutius reads the calendars you choose, read-only, so that a recording gets its meeting's title and the invitees are offered as names for unknown voices. It never changes anything in your Google account and never responds to invitations.

You sign in with Google from the locutius settings on your own computer. You can disconnect at any time in locutius, and remove its access in your Google account under Third-party apps and services.

locutius is used by its developer and people they know personally. It is not verified by Google, so Google shows a warning on the first sign-in.
