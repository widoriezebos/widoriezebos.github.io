Privacy policy

locutius, last updated 20 October 2026

locutius runs on your own computer. The developer of locutius operates no server for it and receives none of your data.

What locutius reads from Google

When you connect Google Calendar, locutius asks Google for two read-only permissions: the list of your calendars, and the events in them. It also receives your account's email address and Google account identifier, to recognise the account when you sign in again. From the calendars you select it reads, for events from a year ago to a year ahead: titles, dates and times, places, the organiser, and the invitees with their responses. It does not read event descriptions, attachments or conference details.

Where it is kept

Events are saved in your locutius workspace, a folder on your computer. The sign-in credential Google issues is kept in your computer's credential store (the macOS Keychain, Windows Credential Manager, or a private file), not in the workspace. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else by locutius itself.

How it is used

Calendar events are used only inside locutius: to name recordings after their meeting, to offer invitees as names for speakers, and to show the week. If you use the Ask panel or automatic meeting notes, locutius runs the AI assistant you have configured on your computer (Claude Code or Codex), and the parts of your workspace it needs, which can include calendar events, are processed by that assistant under your own agreement with its provider. You choose whether to use these features.

locutius's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to train AI models.

Removing it

Remove the connection in locutius under Settings, Calendar; this deletes the stored credential. Revoke locutius's access in your Google account under Third-party apps and services. Events already saved stay in your workspace until you delete them; deleting the workspace folder deletes everything.
